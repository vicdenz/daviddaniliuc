# David Daniliuc — Systems Journal v0.6

- Mode: redesign overhaul
- Purpose: communicate an infrastructure practice through the visual language of a working architectural drawing
- Palette: rice paper `#f4f0e6`, blue-black `#18283b`, cobalt `#2457d6`, periwinkle `#afc2e6`, blue wash `#dce5f2`
- Typography: DM Sans for all reading and display text; IBM Plex Mono for enlarged technical metadata
- Structure: one continuous newspaper column; hierarchy comes from typography and whitespace rather than bordered sections
- Motion: viewport-native procedural Canvas 2D with live routes, packet traffic, grid intersections, heavier scaffold braces, active nodes, pointer parallax, and generated paper grain
- Post-processing: a 0.55px Gaussian blur plus a 16%-opacity 2.4× pixel-quantized pass, composited over the full-resolution scene
- Preserved: current biography, experience, projects, contact destinations, and single-page route
- Avoid: résumé imitation, boxed sections, horizontal separators, oversized headlines, navigation chrome, terminal cosplay, and fabricated engineering metrics
